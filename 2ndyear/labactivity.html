Why I love Sim-racing

blog post: 9/25/2025

It all started back in Grade 11 when I discovered motorsports. At first, it was just a simple interest, but as I dove deeper, I realized how complex and technical the sport really is. From then on, I became hooked. I started watching different motorsports like the 24 Hours of Le Mans,World Endurance Racing , and eventually, I found Formula One. That was it—I was completely addicted. One day, while browsing for games online, I discovered there was a Formula One game. I immediately downloaded it and played nonstop. That only fueled my passion for motorsports even more. I couldn’t stop playing. The reason I’m so addicted to sim racing is because of the feeling it gives me. Whenever I play, my mind goes blank, my senses shut down, and all I can focus on is driving. It’s a euphoric experience—like flying endlessly behind the wheel. That’s why I love sim racing. My everyday life as an IT student

blog post: 9/23/2025

A Day in My Life as an IT Student Every day as an IT student feels like a mix of routine and surprises. It is not always exciting, but it is my life, and I am learning to enjoy the balance of school, personal time, and growth. I usually start the day by waking up and heading straight to school. Classes can get pretty draining, especially when the topics get technical, but I try my best to keep up. Some days it is coding, other days it is data structures or networking. It can feel overwhelming at times, but I remind myself that all of this is shaping me into the IT professional I want to be. After school, the first thing on my mind is food. Eating and resting a bit helps me recharge from the long hours of lectures. Sometimes I just sit back and let my mind relax before deciding what to do next. What happens after really depends on the day. I either hit the gym for a workout or spend time with my girlfriend. Both are equally important to me. Working out helps me stay active and healthy, while seeing her makes my day instantly better. Having that balance keeps me motivated. Later in the evening, I settle down to study. I go through my notes, practice coding, or review lessons from class. IT is not something you can just memorize. You really have to practice and understand how things work. It is tough sometimes, but I know it is worth the effort. By the end of the day, I am ready to rest. Sleeping feels like hitting the reset button so I can face another day of school, workouts, and everything in between. Life as an IT student can be tiring, but it is also rewarding.
